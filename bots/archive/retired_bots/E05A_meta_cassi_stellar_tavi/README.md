# E05A — Cassi & Issac / Stellar / Tavi roster challenger

Controlled roster experiment based on the user's current leaderboard observation. Gameplay files are copied from the G2 champion unchanged; only draft priority differs. Three-pick maps choose Cassi & Issac, Stellar, and Tavi (four acting units). Additional selections prefer Vendetta and Varg.

Hypothesis: the extra Cassi & Issac body plus ranged camp burst and Tavi's field shopping may raise secured-gem throughput. Counterargument: linked death can erase both pair members and their bags; ranged movement/attack restrictions may offset coverage.

This is an unpromoted challenger. Compare both sides across maps against G2 and record score, wins, deaths, faults, and submitted-versus-collected gems before any promotion decision.
