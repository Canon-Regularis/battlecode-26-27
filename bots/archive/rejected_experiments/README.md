# Rejected challenger archives

Rejected challenger source, configuration, README and local JSON results are retained as ZIP archives here. CAMBC `.cambc` build caches are excluded so binaries can be rebuilt from source. Replay bundles remain in the corresponding experiment folders under `replays/`.

Recent archives include E26 (Varg durability shard), E28 (empty-bag capture owner), E29 (Tavi carrier guard), E30 (threat-avoiding bank route), E32 (nearest-goal navigation), E33 (threat-gated pair retreat) and E34 (nearest-step scan). E32 lost its corrected eight-match E05A pilot 1/8 and submitted 4,520 fewer gems. E06 improved the altar-denial suite but regressed against G2 and the starter, did not improve hunter scoring, and increased linked-pair deaths. E33 went 2/8 against the hunter and 3/8 against altar denial in its four-map screen. E34 had no per-turn fuel evidence and did not establish a matched competitive improvement. See `bots/larpbot/EVOLUTION_LOG.md`, `COMPETITIVE_BENCHMARKS.md` and `STRATEGY_MAP_BREAKDOWN.md` for the measured results.

All rejected challenger folders (E05B, E10–E25, E27, E30 and E31) have also been archived with their source, README and results; corrected-cap E05B/E30 results and E31 diagnostic logs are included in separate archives. The remaining unpromoted, specialist and superseded working directories were moved to `bots/archive/retired_bots/` on 14 October 2026. CAMBC build caches were excluded from these ZIP files; replay bundles remain under `replays/`.

E02 `bots/opponents/economic_farmer_proxy` is intentionally retained as a benchmark proxy. The current canonical G3 bot remains at `bots/larpbot`.
