## Why it struggled

1. **Early combat losses precede economic collapse on tight maps.** In the [Brakence collision replay](https://2026a.battlecode.co.uk/game/visualiser/fbf6fb65-ee11-483b-b14f-b7de5134082e), the visualiser highlights a 13-round fight during rounds 36–58 in which Brakence eliminated two Green Burger champions. At round 223, Green Burger had captured 10 camps versus 11, but had banked **0** gems versus 286 and had **0** champion kills versus 3. The 0–1042 final score is therefore not explained by failure to reach camps alone. In the [Quantum corners replay](https://2026a.battlecode.co.uk/game/visualiser/57018959-6132-4104-860c-68c11ed30b5d), the opponent eliminated two champions during rounds 38–54. By round 162, camp captures were 5 versus 16, kills 0 versus 3, and banked gems 94 versus 440. That game finished 94–1028.
2. **The active policy cannot fight or farm while carrying even one gem.** `fast_policy.Bot.play_turn()` calls `bank(me)` and returns whenever `me.gem_count() > 0`. This prevents carrier self-defense and additional camp clearing until the gem is submitted. The policy has no threat evaluation, escort, retreat, or combat response on that path. The match statistics establish a banking problem; the exact share caused by killed carriers versus pathing or altar access requires a deeper action-level replay review.
3. **Camp and combat choices are local and uncoordinated.** The bot moves toward all known camp spawn tiles without team assignments; each champion chooses its own nearest reachable target. The documented shared objective protocol and richer policy are disabled. It attacks a visible enemy if in basic range but otherwise continues camp farming; there is no deliberate pursuit, focus fire, or reaction to a coordinated enemy attack. The early replay fight highlights are consistent with this gap, though they do not isolate its effect from roster or positioning.
4. **Altar handling can miss opportunities.** `bank()` skips every altar with a visible lock using `vision.has_altar_lock()`, which includes friendly locks according to the SDK docs. A friendly lock should permit submission. This is a code-level defect worth fixing, but the available replay summaries do not establish how often it affected these 20 games.

The live matches demonstrate that v5 is not yet competitive with these sampled teams, including the rank 283 opponent. The local D1/proxy benchmarks in `BENCHMARKS.md` did not predict this level of online loss. Prioritize banking reliability and carrier survival, then close-map combat and coordinated camp allocation. Use the same four match links and map pool for subsequent A/B checks.
