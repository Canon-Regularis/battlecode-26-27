# Draft and shopping

Source: `draft.cpp` and `economy.cpp`. Draft candidates are Heart of Ruin, Marcella, Vesper Vale, Ren, Vendetta, Varg, Ryth, Stellar, Prism, Willow, Tavi and Cassi & Issac, taking the first available legal option. Normal local matches draft the first five. Linked-body/fallback matches remain an explicit coverage gap.

Buy legally while dead, at spawn, or as Tavi in the field; avoid interrupting channels. Buy five durability-health shards first (Vesper starts treasure-strength), then five monster-focus shards for caster types or monster-strength shards for other types. Later phases alternate power damage stat and power health. Combined emblem counts contribute to phase completion. Purchase loops are bounded at 40 actions and stop before the fuel reserve. Buy an altar lock when available and none is held.

A safe empty-bag body with >=300 gold may recall for equipment. This keeps the opening build from depending on death to spend gold. The release is a simple fixed build rather than a matchup-specific economy optimizer.
